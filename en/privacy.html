Car Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Car Ledger (“the app”) is for recording your own fuel, car wash, supplies, maintenance and insurance costs. We care about your privacy. This page explains how the app handles data.

We collect no data

  • The app does not send any of your data to the developer or to any third party.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you.
  • The developer cannot access anything you record in the app.

Where your data is stored

  • By default, data is stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.
  • Syncing is your choice and entirely optional:
  • iCloud: data is kept in the iCloud space of your own Apple ID, managed by Apple and subject to Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.
  • Google Sheets: data is sent to a Google Apps Script that you create and deploy, and written to your own Google Sheet, subject to Google's privacy policy. The developer cannot access it, and it never passes through any server run by the developer.
  • When syncing with Google Sheets, the API token you paste is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain.
  • App settings such as language, units and currency are stored only on your iPhone.
  • You can switch or turn off syncing any time in Settings → Sync Method.

What you can do

  • View and edit: view, edit or delete any record in the app at any time.
  • Delete all data: deleting the app removes the data on your phone. If you synced, also delete the corresponding file or content in your iCloud or Google Sheet.

Children

  • The app is not directed at children and does not collect anyone's personal information.

Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

  • If you have any questions, email: yichenallapp@gmail.com
