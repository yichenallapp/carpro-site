Car Ledger Support

Having a problem or a suggestion? Check the questions below first; if you can't find an answer, email us.

Where is my data stored?

By default only on your iPhone. In Settings → Sync Method you can choose to sync to iCloud or your own Google Sheet, or not sync at all. Deleting the app also deletes the data on the phone, so turn on syncing first if you want to keep it.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings → Sync Method, choose iCloud Drive (or Google Sheets) and wait until syncing finishes. On the new phone, install the app, sign in with the same Apple ID and choose iCloud Drive too. Cars with the same plate merge automatically and records fill in for each other.

How do I sync with Google Sheets?

The app has a step-by-step guide (the welcome screen on first launch, or the setup guide next to Settings → Sync Method). It takes about 5 minutes: create the spreadsheet and tabs, paste the code the app provides into Apps Script, generate an API token, deploy as a web app, then paste the URL and token back into the app. A computer is easier.

How should I name the tabs and headers?

Traditional Chinese, English and Simplified Chinese are all supported; use one set per spreadsheet (English is not case-sensitive). In English: Fuel, Car Wash, Supplies, Maintenance, Insurance. The in-app guide shows the right names for your language along with copyable header rows.

Test Connection says “unauthorized”

The token was pasted incorrectly or has extra text. Copy it again. Running setupToken again creates a new token and the old one stops working.

It says a tab wasn't found

Tab names must match the examples (English is not case-sensitive). A spreadsheet that already has older data works fine.

Can I change units and currency?

Yes, in Settings → Units & Currency. Units: km/L, L/100km or mpg (US or UK); currency: 18 choices. This only changes how values are shown and entered: data is always stored in kilometers and liters, and amounts you've already recorded aren't converted.

Can I track more than one car?

Yes. Each car has its own plate, model, purchase date and starting odometer. Tap the plate at the top left of Home, Records or Stats to switch. Several cars can share one spreadsheet, told apart by the “車牌” (plate) column.

How is average fuel economy calculated?

It uses the distance from the odometer at purchase (or the first fill-up with an odometer reading) to the last reading, divided by the fuel added over that period. Enter the odometer when you fill up. You can also switch to the economy of the last two fill-ups.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone's language by default. You can pick one in Settings → Language; it takes effect after you reopen the app. Supported: English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
